> [!IMPORTANT]
> **How to Use This Worksheet:**
> - Under PYROJA's hardened safety architecture, all products without an approved explicit rule enforce a pack multiple of `1` on wholesale orders. This ensures orders are **never** blocked by unverified heuristics.
> - This worksheet provides **verified historical sales evidence** from 3,590 eligible FoxPro wholesale transactions across 59 commercial debtor accounts (`NAMEMST.TCODE == 'SDEB'`). Retail cash counter orders (`99999`) have been excluded.
> - **Approval Policy Standard**: Recommendations for approval require **strictly 100.0% divisibility** across at least 2 distinct wholesale customers and 3 lines. Divisors and GCD are reported as empirical evidence.
> - **Action Required:** For each product, review the historical sales evidence and recommendation, then mark your decision in the **Final Business-Owner Sign-Off** field:
>   - `[ ] Approve` (enforce the recommended pack multiple)
>   - `[ ] Change: ___` (specify a custom wholesale multiple)
>   - `[ ] Reject` (maintain loose single-unit sales, pack multiple = 1)
> - Once signed off, approved rules are recorded in `sync-service/app/data/catalog_pack_rules.json` with `status: 'APPROVED'`.
